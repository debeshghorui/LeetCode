Delete your MobLeet account

This page is for the MobLeet Android app (com.debeshghorui.mobleet).

In the app

  1. Open MobLeet and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is permanent. It removes your account, profile, submissions, test results, and solved progress. Problem content that is not yours is kept.

If you cannot open the app

Email the developer address on the Google Play listing with the subject “Delete my MobLeet account” and the email you used to sign in. The same data listed above will be deleted.
