MobLeet privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026

MobLeet is an independent coding-practice app. It is not affiliated with LeetCode. The Android application id is com.debeshghorui.mobleet.

Information we collect

When you continue with Google or GitHub, we receive the email address, display name, and profile image those providers share, along with a user id. We store the code you submit, the language, the result, and which problems you have solved. A session token is stored on your device so you stay signed in. We also send crash and usage diagnostics that include your user id, problem id, language, result, and timing. Those diagnostics do not include your source code.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, show your profile, save submissions, show your progress, and keep the app reliable. MobLeet does not show ads and does not sell your information.

Where it is stored

Account and practice data are stored with Supabase. Sign-in is handled by Google or GitHub through Supabase Auth. Crash reports go to Sentry. Product usage events go to PostHog.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and practice data until you delete your account. Deletion removes your user, profile, submissions, test results, and solved progress.

Delete your account

Open MobLeet, go to Profile, and tap Delete account. You can also follow the account deletion page.

Children

MobLeet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Use the developer email address shown on the MobLeet listing in Google Play.
